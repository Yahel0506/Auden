## New

- **Auden DJ.** A new tab in the middle of the bar: a DJ that opens sets for you, to come back to, to discover, for the moment and from your own music, and talks between songs. Each session varies from the last, and it has its own animated cover on the mini players, the notification and the lock screen. The tab can be removed in Settings.
- **A redesigned Library.** Replay is a slim banner that leads to your stats, "Respaldo musical" opens every song your cache holds, Downloads and Local music are playlists with their own covers, and a new "From Spotify" row lists your Spotify playlists — tap its name to open Spotify.
- **Apple Music style player.** A glass navigation bar with search built in that folds into the open tab, a mini player that morphs into the player, a flowing backdrop behind lyrics and the queue, queue modes as pills, and a compact audio output menu.
- **Sync device playlists** to a YouTube Music playlist, and see which service each is synced with.
- **What's new** has its own section at the top of Settings, with the installed version.
