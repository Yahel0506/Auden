## New

- **Repair metadata.** The ⋮ menu of every playlist and folder, and of each downloaded or device song, can repair the tags of its files. Any missing title, artist, album or cover is filled in from the song's own record and written into the file. Lyrics are left alone.
- **Upgrade a download.** A downloaded song now offers *Upgrade quality* in the player. On Wi-Fi it streams the best copy available, so the superior version can be heard over Wi-Fi while the download stays on the device.
- **What's new.** Settings now shows the notes for the version you have installed, in your language.
