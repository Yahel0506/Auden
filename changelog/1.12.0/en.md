## Highlights

Everything new from **BitChord v1.9.1**, and a player that looks and behaves the way it should. Pick how the now-playing background looks, hide the little lyric line and the volume bar without the layout falling apart, and let your downloaded playlists keep themselves up to date. AutoMix analyses songs sooner and mixes more of them as real loops.

## Player

- **Three backgrounds.** Choose under Settings > Home: **Continuation** (the default, a soft mesh that carries on the colors of the cover), **Colors** (the classic mesh gradient, back again) or **Blur** (the cover, blurred across the whole screen).
- **A softer Continuation.** The mesh gets blurrier toward the bottom and darker too, so the buttons keep their contrast while the top stays bright.
- **A gentler fade** where the cover dissolves into the background, along a smooth curve instead of a hard line.
- **Tighter controls.** The scrubber, transport and volume sit closer together, and the cover takes the height they give back.
- **Hide mini lyrics.** Under Settings > Miscellaneous, remove the one-line synced lyric above the progress bar. The controls share its space evenly and the title does not move.
- **Hide volume bar** now does the same: the remaining controls share its height instead of leaving a hole.
- **Disable blend effects** shows the player's icons, text and menus in plain white if the tinted look isn't for you.

## Home

- **One place for Home settings.** *Content Preferences* is now **Home**, and it also holds the Home mix slider and the shelves you can turn on or off, which used to sit under Appearance.
- **Hide what you don't use.** Hide Recents, videos, Top Picks for You or Find Your Mood from Home, and show music videos as square thumbnails.
- The large hero cards at the top of Home are gone, as in BitChord 1.9.1.

## Motion artwork

- **Two switches instead of one.** **Animated cover** covers the looping artwork from Apple Music, Tidal and the community index. **Background video** covers Spotify's short vertical Canvas, with its setup right below it.
- **Cellular data** applies to both, so a clip never loops over mobile data unless you allow it.
- **Full-screen covers** on albums and playlists, running to the edges of the page.

## Playlists and downloads

- **Downloaded playlists update themselves.** When a playlist you downloaded gains songs, the new ones are downloaded too. That covers YouTube Music playlists and the device copies of Spotify and YouTube Music playlists, as well as the ones you made yourself. It is on by default under *Keep playlists updated*, respects Wi-Fi only, and asks before fetching more than 50 songs at once.

## AutoMix

- **Analysis keeps up.** Songs are analysed starting with the one playing and the one after it, and work for songs you skipped past is dropped, so a mix is ready sooner.
- **The new song no longer runs fast.** When a track enters sped up to match the tempo, it now eases back to its own speed while the old one is still fading out, instead of seconds afterwards.
- **More loop mixes.** Line-synced lyrics without an end time no longer rule out a loop the vocal model finds clean, so more pairs get the pattern-over-pattern mix.
- **Automix overview.** The song menu shows the transition coming up.

## Playback

- **Music only.** *Prefer music-only version* plays music videos as their audio, and converts the next track ahead of time so AutoMix and crossfade keep working.
- **Better song matching.** Direct Source Stream checks length and artist before playing, so it no longer lands on a different song or a 30-second preview.
- **Bit-perfect.** No more dropouts with the screen off on direct USB and AAudio exclusive, and the DAC is released during long pauses and resumes from the exact spot.

## Updates

- **Update notices say Auden.** The dialog reads *Auden x.y.z is available to download*.
- **What's new in your language.** The notes shown before you update come straight from the release and follow the language you set in the app, falling back to English.

## Fixes and polish

- Listen Together keeps its built-in server when the setting is blank, so Create and Join are no longer disabled in release builds.
- Less lag while the player slides over a page.
- A lighter frost for Liquid Glass on phones that struggle with the full effect, and a quality picker for addons that offer more than one.
- The search field stays readable on glass, and loading placeholders are refreshed.
- General stability fixes across the app.
