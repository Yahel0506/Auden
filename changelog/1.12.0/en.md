## Highlights

This release brings in everything new from **BitChord v1.9.1** and reshapes the player around it. The now-playing background has three looks to choose from, the cover melts into it more gently, and Home settings finally have a place of their own.

## Player

- **Three backgrounds.** Pick the one you like under Settings > Home: **Continuation** (the default, a soft mesh that carries on the colors of the cover), **Colors** (the classic mesh gradient, back again) or **Blur** (the cover, blurred across the whole screen).
- **A softer Continuation.** The mesh gets blurrier toward the bottom and darker too, so the buttons keep their contrast without the colors going dull at the top.
- **A gentler fade.** The cover now dissolves into the background along a smooth curve instead of a hard line.
- **Tighter controls.** Transport, scrubber and volume sit closer together, and the cover takes the height they give back.
- **Disable blend effects.** Shows the player's icons, text and menus in plain white if the tinted look isn't for you.

## Home

- **One place for Home settings.** *Content Preferences* is now simply **Home**, and it also holds the Home mix slider and the shelves you can turn on or off, which used to sit under Appearance.
- **Hide what you don't use.** Hide Recents, videos, Top Picks for You or Find Your Mood from Home, and show music videos as square thumbnails.

## Motion artwork

- **Two switches instead of one.** **Animated cover** covers the looping artwork from Apple Music, Tidal and the community index. **Background video** covers Spotify's short vertical Canvas, with its setup right below it.
- **Cellular data** applies to both, so a clip never loops over mobile data unless you say so.
- **Full-screen covers** on albums and playlists, running to the edges of the page.

## Playback

- **Music only.** *Prefer music-only version* plays music videos as their audio, and converts the next track ahead of time so Automix and crossfade keep working.
- **Automix overview.** The song menu shows the transition coming up.
- **Better song matching.** Direct Source Stream now checks length and artist before playing, so it no longer lands on a different song or a 30-second preview.
- **Bit-perfect.** No more dropouts with the screen off on direct USB and AAudio exclusive, and the DAC is released during long pauses and resumes from the exact spot.

## Updates

- **Update notices say Auden.** The dialog now reads *Auden x.y.z is available to download*.
- **What's new in your language.** The notes shown before you update come straight from the release and follow the language you have set in the app, falling back to English when there is none.

## Fixes and polish

- Less lag while the player slides over a page.
- A lighter frost for Liquid Glass on phones that struggle with the full effect.
- A quality picker for addons that offer more than one.
- The search field stays readable on glass, and loading placeholders are refreshed.
- General stability fixes across the app.
