## Player

- **Readable lyrics and queue.** Their backdrop now dims only the colours bright enough to make the text hard to read, instead of darkening everything. It uses fewer, larger pools of colour so the movement can be seen, and keeps a cover's accent colour — gold lettering on a black sleeve stays gold — while dark colours that belong to the cover are left alone.
- **Livelier moving backdrop.** With an animated backdrop the colours below the cover differ a little more from one another as they drift, always meeting the cover's own edge.
- **Detail pages.** Albums, playlists, folders and artists carry the cover's colours on below it, fading into the page.
- **Loop mixes.** A mix no longer stops playback when the next song is shorter than the mix and starts late.

## Home

- **Recommendations without an account.** Signed out, Home adds shelves built from what you play in Auden: "Because you listened to…", "More like…" and "Best of…", drawn from YouTube Music's catalogue around your most played songs and artists.

## Library

- **Select many songs.** Every list of songs can be put in a selection: from a song's menu ("Select songs"), from a release's ⋯ menu, or by holding a song in Downloads or Cached songs. A floating bar shows the count, selects all and opens the song menu for everything picked.
- **Filing a selection into playlists.** A playlist shows as ticked only when it already holds every picked song; ticking an empty one adds just the songs it lacks, and unticking one takes them all out — asking first when that is more than one.
- **Weekly recap.** The statistics banner now shows your weekly recap over a green, blue and violet mesh, and Replay opens on "This week".
- **Signed out.** The empty "YT Music" row under your lists is gone; only the notice to sign in is left at the bottom.
- **A little more room.** The text rows (Downloads, Local music, Cached songs, Other) are slightly taller with larger type, there is more space above and below the statistics banner and between pinned lists and those rows, and pinned lists carry a smaller title.
- **Full-bleed covers on device folders.** Downloads, Local music and Cached songs now follow the "full-screen cover" setting and dissolve into the same colour continuation as albums, playlists and artists.
- **Covers.** Pick a photo (with a cropper), remove the cover, or make one in the new cover creator: gradient, mesh, aurora, sunburst, stripes, waves, dots, ripple and grain backgrounds; text with fonts, effects and a full colour picker; labels; stickers in five styles; and pictures with a single filter each. Covers you set survive syncs of the playlist they came from.
- **Edit playlist.** Name, cover and description in one sheet. The playlist menu is shorter: related options sit behind "Lyrics and tags" and "Manage playlist", and "Add local files" is no longer in it.
- **A flow to make or import a playlist.** Choose to create or import, then where to keep it (this device or YouTube Music). You can import from a file, from Spotify, or from YouTube Music — now also by pasting a link, so it works without being signed in.

## Settings

- **Reorganised.** The sections are the same, but their options are grouped in headed blocks with a summary on each section, more distinct icons (Auden DJ uses the DJ icon from the bar) and clearer wording.
- **Pictures instead of words.** Theme, player theme and backdrop motion are picked from small drawings; full-screen and album covers, square thumbnails, Automix, loudness and skip silence have an illustration under them.
- **Lyrics blur preview.** The blur setting shows lyrics with the real blur you chose, so you can judge it before opening the player.

## Look & Glass

- **Menus and alerts.** Menu pages open with a slide inside the same panel, and "Manage download" is one of them, so it also opens from the player. The menu that opens on a hold and the account switcher use the alerts' glass.
- **App icons.** The old BitChord icons — Monochrome and the Neon family — are gone. Default and Liquid Glass (light and dark) remain; if you were using a retired one, you are moved to Default.
