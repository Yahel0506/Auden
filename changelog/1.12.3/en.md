## New

- **Assume a lossless output.** A new switch under *Sources* serves lossless-only addons even when Auden doesn't recognise a lossless output, for a DAC, streamer or Bluetooth codec that Android reports in an unusual way. Off by default; with it on, the audio may still play through a lossy route.

## Fixes

- Each device keeps its own volume when playback is handed over between Auden devices.
