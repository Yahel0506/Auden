## Library

- **Statistics banner on top.** Replay's banner is back at the very top of the page, now in gold instead of purple, and the Statistics row is gone.
- **Pinned lists.** The grid under the banner now shows only what you pinned: at most six, three to a row, centred when a row isn't full, each with just its name and no pin mark.
- **Pin anything.** Hold any playlist, album, artist or podcast card — or one of your device lists — to pin it. The limit is six, and pins whose list no longer shows anywhere are dropped by themselves instead of counting unseen. Holding an artist now opens a menu too.
- **Fewer, smaller text rows.** Downloads, Local music, Cached songs and a new "Other" row, with blue icons and about a third less height. WebDAV and SMB keep their rows once set up. "Other" opens your Artists, Albums, Podcasts and Subscriptions as rows of cards, like the playlist rows.
- **My lists.** A two-column grid with every list kept on this device, above the YT Music and Spotify rows.
- **Create a playlist from the top.** A "+" beside the history button in the profile pill asks for the kind: on this device, on YouTube Music, import from Spotify or import from a file. The create tiles in the YT Music row are gone.

## Playlists

- **Local files in any playlist.** "Add local files" is in every playlist's menu and opens a file picker. Files added to a YouTube Music or Spotify playlist stay on this device: they are never sent to the account, survive syncs and keep their access after a restart, and can be removed from the song menu.
- **Local music in any device list.** Adding a song from a file now lists every playlist on this device, saved copies and imports included, so files and streamed songs can sit in the same list. Syncs with Spotify and YouTube Music don't remove them.

## Player

- **Where it's playing.** The output button shows what the sound comes out of: buds, headphones, a speaker, a car, a TV, a watch, Cast, or another Auden device — a computer for a browser, a phone, a TV. The phone's own speaker keeps the usual icon. With another device playing, the line under the controls names it.
- **Headphones in the output menu.** USB and unrecognised Bluetooth devices used to show a cable-like glyph; they now use headphones.
- **No "Queue" title** above the queue; the clear button stays at the right.

## Look

- **One glass.** Every glass surface now has the navigation bar's: the top bar's pills, the tab bar, the player's card, alerts, dialogs, pickers, the mini player, the volume HUD and menus, with the same milky tint and lit rim.
- **Titles level with the profile pill.** Home, Explore, Library and DJ carry their title in the first row of the list, centred on the same line as the pill, with more room below. It scrolls away with the page and the top fade waits until you scroll, so it stays crisp at rest.
