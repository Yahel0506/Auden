## Auden, new look

- **A new name on the mark.** A new Auden logo everywhere — the app, the TV and Wear OS apps, the website and the browser extension — and thirteen launcher icons to pick from: Liquid Glass in light and dark, black, white and nine colours.
- **A launch animation.** The logo's liquid-glass animation replaces the static splash, in light and dark.
- **A welcome to the new look.** Shown once, the first time you open the app after updating: four pages in black with the logo's loop of drops behind them.
- **Your colour, everywhere.** Pick an accent in Settings and it colours the open tab, switches, banners and the stats mesh.
- **Auden on Home.** The name now sits beside the mark at the top of Home.

## Glass & Menus

- **A reworked nav bar.** The film is clearer through the middle line and thicker along the top and bottom edges, with a thin rim, more saturated colour and a gentler lens. The drop you drag across the bar is smaller and darker-edged, and every tab inside it takes the accent colour.
- **Clean tap on navigation tabs.** Tapping a tab in the navigation bar no longer shows an overlay darkening or ripple, letting the liquid drop glide cleanly without visual interference.
- **Glass menus.** Menus throughout the app now use the liquid glass material with refined rounding.
- **Restructured song menu.** The ⋯ song menu is reorganized into clear categories with smooth back navigation for nested menus like playlists and details.
- **Opacity slider.** Settings has a Glass opacity slider from 0 to 100% (75% by default): the more see-through the glass, the more it blurs and the more muted its colours.
- **Glass or Solid.** One selector in Appearance replaces the Liquid Glass switch, Progressive Blur and "Reduce dynamic blur": Glass is the default, and Solid fills every surface instead of sampling it. The glass performance mode is gone.
- **Library icon.** The Auden mark is cut out of the Library tab's icon, as in Apple Music.
- **Smoother player close.** Letting go of the player after dragging it down no longer darkens the page for a frame.

## Auden DJ

- **Moods and shelves.** Browse and discover DJ sets through dedicated mood categories and shelves directly on the DJ screen.
- **Save sessions as playlists.** Turn any DJ session into a playlist in your library to revisit and replay whenever you want.
- **Natural pronunciation.** A built-in pronunciation engine ensures artist names, song titles, and foreign words are spoken naturally by the DJ.
- **Smarter request parser.** A sturdier request parser that understands varied, natural phrasing when asking the DJ for music.

## Library & Account

- **Likes without an account.** Favorite and like songs without signing into any account, stored locally on your device.
- **"Favorites" playlist.** An automatic favorites collection with its own custom cover art.
- **Account nickname.** Set your display nickname in Account settings for your profile and Listen Together parties.

## Connect

- **No Google account needed.** Devices find each other through your Spotify account or the same network.
- **What you start stays where it plays.** A song, album or playlist started from your phone or the web keeps playing on the device that was already playing, instead of taking the session over.

## Updates

- **An update page.** An update opens a page with the logo's loop, the version and the whole changelog, with the download button and its progress always in view. The download carries on in the background when you close the page. The same page shows "What's new in this update" in Settings.
- **A fuller icon.** The update icon on Home is a solid disc with a bold arrow.
