## Highlights

**Play on your other devices.** Open Auden on your phone and on the web with the same account, press Audio output, and every device with Auden open is in the list. Pick one and the music moves there, from the same second. While it plays somewhere else, the player on the other devices shows exactly the same song, queue, lyrics and progress, and every button drives it.

## Connect

- **One session, every device.** The queue, the position, shuffle, repeat and play/pause are the same everywhere; only the device making sound changes.
- **Control from anywhere.** Play, pause, skip, seek, volume, shuffle, repeat, and queue edits (play next, add to queue, remove, reorder, clear) work from any device, including the notification and lock screen.
- **Shuffle keeps its place.** Turning shuffle off on any device brings back the same list everywhere.
- **Off if you want.** *Settings > Play on my other devices*.

## Web

- **The Auden web player** now carries the Auden features: your local library, playlists, lyrics tools, Spotify library, Home shelves, the DJ with its animated ring and moving gradient, player backgrounds, offline copies and backups.
- The **DJ** shows its screen only while it speaks; when a song plays you get the normal player.

## Fixes and polish

- The built-in Listen Together and Connect servers are Auden's own.
