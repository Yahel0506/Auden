## Fixes

- **Devices find each other.** The phone and the web player looked for each other in different rooms, so *Auden devices* only ever listed the device you were on. They now meet in one room keyed on the YouTube channel's @handle (or the Google login's email when there is none), which both read the same way, so every device signed in to the same account shows up in Audio output. Update the app and open the web player again.
- The web player says what is missing when it can't look devices up.
